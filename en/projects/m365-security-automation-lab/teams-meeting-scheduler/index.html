Automatic meeting scheduling from Teams

From a Teams message to a confirmed meeting, in seconds.

In my Microsoft 365 lab, I set up a Power Automate flow that removes the back-and-forth of booking a meeting. From any Teams message, one click on “Automatically schedule a meeting with the sender” opens a short form: subject, invitation message and duration. The flow does the rest.

Demo

How it works

  1. 01 · Trigger

    An action added to the Teams message menu.

  2. 02 · Processing

    The flow sets the duration and working hours, queries the Outlook calendars of the organizer and the sender, then picks the first common slot.

  3. 03 · Result

    The meeting is created in Outlook and a confirmation card is posted in Teams (date, time, attendees, message).
