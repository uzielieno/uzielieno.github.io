Multi-Agent AI Orchestration

Monitoring agent

Watch, filter, surface only what matters.

The monitoring agent produces a brief with fixed sections: market and AI, a fintech’s competitors, conferences and deadlines, content creators worth contacting, decisions to make. It publishes nothing and contacts no one.

Automated actions

  1. Daily · 7:00 amFlash brief: 300 words max, the essentials of the day
  2. Daily · 8:30 amChange tracking on a fintech’s website
  3. Monday · 8:15 amIn-depth weekly review: 650 words max
  4. Monday · 8:15 amPress and social media review of the same fintech

How it works

Sources

  • Every fact carries its source (URL)
  • Impact explained, with a confidence level: certain, likely or to be checked

Memory

  • State files kept from one brief to the next
  • A fact already reported does not come back without news
  • Open topics recalled at D+30 and D+60

Format

  • Strict word cap
  • A section with nothing real takes one line
  • 0 to 2 concrete decisions proposed

Guardrails

  • Read-only toward the outside: never posts, comments, signs up or buys.
  • Without a verifiable source, information is marked “unverified”.
  • No personal data collected, apart from professional contact details published by the people themselves.
  • Immediate escalation to the manager: deadline under 30 days, short-window opportunity.
